This solution automates cross-region replication of S3 bucket configurations using Step Functions and Lambda, copying bucket policies, lifecycle rules, encryption, tags, and access controls, and logging operations to DynamoDB for audit trails. It suits organizations consolidating buckets post-merger or meeting data-residency compliance requirements.
